North Eastern GSD Club Championship Show 2026

It is with great honor that I accepted the invitation to the North Eastern GSD Club, as it is always a pleasure to meet my British friends who are passionate about German Shepherds. I spent a very pleasant day thanks to the kindness and typically British sportsmanship of the exhibitors and owners.The level of the competitors was very good, and I would like to congratulate the breeders who are doing a wonderful job of selection despite the distance from the country of origin. As in most countries, the quality of the females is superior to that of the males. I noted a few subjects at the limit of what is acceptable in terms of size. In a few rare subjects, a somewhat overly marked rear angulation was present. Great caution regarding these two criteria is strongly recommended. Despite unauthorized double handling, most subjects performed well in their gaits.Finally, I warmly thank Claire Starkey and her entire team, without whom this charming day could not have taken place.
